Weekly Summer Community Yoga Classes with Yoga Habit!

The Francisville Recreation Center is pleased to once again partner with Yoga Habit to host a weekly community yoga class! Starting June 23rd, Yoga Habit instructors will teach a weekly community yoga class at every Tuesday evening at 6pm! After class, students get to enjoy a private free swim session from 7pm until 7:45pm!

To sign up, visit Yoga Habit's official calendar.

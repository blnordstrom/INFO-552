Francisville Pool

The City of Philadelphia's pools open on June 13th!

All open pools will provide free swim lessons for children, making it easier than ever to stay safe and confident in the water this summer. Swim programming is also part of the city’s summer camps, giving thousands of Philly kids the chance to learn and play. Check back with us on June 13th for our summer swim class schedule!

Every city pool will offer daily public swim from 1:00p.m. to 4:00p.m., with some sites offering extended hours depending on staffing. Since schedules vary, you are encouraged to check your neighborhood pool or use the city’s Pool Finder app to stay up to date.
